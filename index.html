Built for researchers

AI proposes. The researcher decides.

The product is designed around evidence, privacy, purpose limitation and explicit human approval.

01

Evidence first

Major identity claims and signature objects can point back to the paper, repository, project or profile that supports them.

02

Private & purpose-limited

Uploads remain private by default. Without your explicit permission, we do not publish, disclose, sell or redistribute them, use them for unrelated purposes, or use private uploads to train shared or public AI models.

03

You control your data

You decide which sources are used, what appears publicly, and whether a digital profile exists. You can request deletion of uploaded material and generated profiles.

04

Human approval

Nothing goes into a final identity or physical production until the researcher or authorized customer reviews and approves it.

↳
ResearcherDNA privacy commitment

We commit not to publish, disclose, sell or distribute your uploaded materials, and not to use them beyond the ResearcherDNA experience you requested — including training shared or public AI models — without your explicit authorization. If you suspect any leak, misuse or unauthorized disclosure, please report it immediately to lahoule.lee@gmail.com.
